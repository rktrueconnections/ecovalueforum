The idea

Environmental progress does not have to depend on sacrifice, philanthropy or regulation alone. The most scalable solutions are often those that create economic value while solving environmental problems.

What we believe

Make the solution
the profitable choice.

01

Profit drives scale.

When solving an environmental problem creates a viable business, the solution can attract customers, entrepreneurs and investment and grow far beyond a pilot.

02

Waste can become value.

Materials, energy and resources currently treated as waste can become inputs, products and new sources of economic value.

03

Technology enables the transition.

Israeli technology and entrepreneurship can turn environmental challenges into commercially viable businesses and solutions.
